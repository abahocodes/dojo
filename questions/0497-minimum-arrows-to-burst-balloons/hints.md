# Hints

## Hint 1
Consider the balloon whose right end is smallest. Some arrow must hit it.
Where is the best place for that arrow?

## Hint 2
Shooting at that balloon's right end is never worse than shooting anywhere
further left inside it: every balloon hit further left that is still
unburst is also hit at the right end.

## Hint 3
Sort by right end. Fire at the first balloon's end; skip every balloon whose
start is at or before that position; fire again at the end of the first
balloon that starts after it.
